Statutory Authorities & Legislative Acts

All threshold guardrails in this platform derive directly from national Zimbabwean statutory acts and international ISO accreditation frameworks.

Environmental Management Act [Chapter 20:27]
Principal legislation governing all environmental protection, pollution control, and natural resource stewardship in Zimbabwe.
  • SI 268 of 2007 (Effluent and Solid Waste Disposal): Classifies discharges into Blue (Clean), Green (Safe), Amber (High Attention), and Red (Hazardous). Mandates discharge limits (pH 6.0 - 9.0, conductivity 1000 μS/cm, heavy metal ppm).
  • SI 72 of 2009 (Atmospheric Pollution Control): Establishes emission boundaries for smoke, dust, and respirable silica (quartz limit 2.00 mg/m³).
  • SI 12 of 2007 (Hazardous Substances, Pesticides and Toxic Substances): Controls cyanide, toxic reagents, and secondary bunding requirements.
Ministry of Mines & Mining Development Legislation
  • Mines and Minerals Act [Chapter 21:05]: Governs statutory reporting, safety returns, and prospecting rights.
  • Mining (Management and Safety) Regulations (SI 109 of 1990): Outlines hoisting ropes, bench stability, shaft signals, blasting procedures, and mandatory immediate reporting of fatal/serious incidents.
  • Mining (Health and Sanitation) Regulations (SI 182 of 1995): Sets water and sanitation standards for industrial mining camps.
National Social Security Authority (NSSA) & ISO Accreditations
  • Factories and Works Act [Chapter 14:08]: Statutory inspection mandates for steam boilers, pressure vessels, and overhead gantry cranes.
  • Pneumoconiosis Act [Chapter 15:08]: Mandatory chest X-rays and medical fitness certificates for workers in dusty trades.
  • Standards Association of Zimbabwe (SAZ): ZWS ISO 45001:2018 (Occupational Health and Safety) and ZWS ISO 9001:2015 (Quality Management).
